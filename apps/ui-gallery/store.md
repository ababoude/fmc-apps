A tour of the stock firmware's LVGL widgets as an app can use them: lists, buttons, switches, gauges, a time picker and the built-in fonts. Tap a section to open it, tap its title or **Back** to return; the crown scrolls.

Mostly a reference for people writing their own apps.

## Requirements

The [patched firmware](/firmware) and the [App Loader](/firmware). Open it from the **Apps** row at the end of the watch's app list (list style, not the waterfall).
